Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

TLDR

FitLink collects only the data needed to connect coaches with clients and deliver fitness services. We never sell your data. You can delete your account at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile photo when you create an account.
  • Health & Fitness Data: With your explicit permission, we access Apple Health or Google Health Connect data including steps, heart rate, active energy, sleep, and workout metrics.
  • Workout & Nutrition Data: Exercise logs, diet plans, progress photos, and assessment data entered by you or your coach.
  • Payment Information: Payment details are processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Communications: Messages exchanged between coaches and clients within the app.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and push notification tokens for delivering notifications.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and maintain the FitLink service
  • To connect clients with their coaches
  • To display health metrics and track fitness progress
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To send push notifications about workouts, messages, and reminders
  • To improve our app and user experience

3. Health Data

Health data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect is:

  • Only accessed with your explicit permission
  • Used solely to display health insights within the app
  • Shared only with your assigned coach (if applicable)
  • Never sold to third parties
  • Never used for advertising or marketing purposes

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only in these limited circumstances:

  • With your coach: Workout data, health metrics, and messages are shared with your assigned fitness coach.
  • Payment processing: Payment data is shared with Stripe for transaction processing.
  • Push notifications: Device tokens are shared with Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) and Expo for notification delivery.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure
  • All data transmission is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Authentication tokens are stored in secure device storage
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke health data access at any time through your device settings

7. Children's Privacy

FitLink is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at:

Email: privacy@getfitlink.com
